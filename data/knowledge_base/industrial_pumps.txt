Centrifugal pumps are widely used in Water Treatment plants to move
process water and effluent. They are also common in Industrial
Processing lines for circulating fluids, in Chemical Dosing systems,
and in HVAC circulation loops. Stainless steel centrifugal pumps are
favored in food & beverage and pharmaceutical settings due to
corrosion resistance and ease of cleaning. Typical selection criteria
include flow rate, head/pressure, NPSH, and material compatibility
with the pumped fluid.

Motors used to drive centrifugal pumps are commonly rated in kW and
are selected with a service factor margin above the pump's hydraulic
power demand. Maximum working pressure is a key safety spec and
should never be exceeded during operation.
